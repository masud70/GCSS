# About GCSS

:::intro
The University of Manitoba Graduate Computer Science Society (GCSS) is a student-governed association representing the graduate students in the Department of Computer Science.
:::

We advocate for graduate students, support their academic and professional growth, and build a community where students from every lab can meet, share their research and collaborate.
