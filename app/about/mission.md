# Our mission

:::intro
GCSS is a student-governed association representing the graduate students in the Department of Computer Science. Its mission rests on six commitments.
:::

:::features
:::feature icon=megaphone
### Representation
Provide graduate student representation at the department level and at the UofM Graduate & Postdoctoral Society (UMGPS) Council.
:::
:::feature icon=graduation
### Academic & professional growth
Support the academic and professional growth of graduate students.
:::
:::feature icon=shield
### Advocacy
Promote graduate student advocacy and make proper use of departmental and external resources to benefit graduate students.
:::
:::feature icon=heart
### Social support
Offer social support to graduate students.
:::
:::feature icon=network
### Collaboration
Provide a supportive environment for all CS graduate students and promote research collaboration between students.
:::
:::feature icon=wallet
### Support for organisers
Provide financial and bureaucratic support to any graduate student who is willing to put in the work to organise an event for GCSS.
:::
:::
