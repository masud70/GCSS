# Graduate *Computer Science* Society

## A home for graduate students in Computer Science.

GCSS represents graduate students in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Manitoba. We run socials, research talks, and bring student concerns to the department. Whether you started this term or are finishing your thesis, you're welcome here.

[Explore the seminar series](events/seminars.md) [Meet the executives](executives/2026-2027.md)
:::

:::features
## What we do

Everything GCSS does comes back to one goal: a better graduate school experience for every CS student.

:::feature icon=megaphone
### Representation
We speak for CS graduate students at the department level and on the UofM Graduate & Postdoctoral Society (UMGPS) Council.
:::
:::feature icon=presentation
### Research talks
The CS Grad Seminar is a low-stakes stage to practise presenting your work to a general CS audience.
:::
:::feature icon=users
### Community
Socials and events that bring students from every lab together, and maybe introduce your next research collaborator.
:::
:::feature icon=wallet
### Support for organisers
Financial and bureaucratic support for any graduate student willing to put in the work to organise an event.
:::
:::

:::cta
## Give a talk at the CS Grad Seminar
Thirty minutes, a friendly room of fellow graduate students, and a line for your CV. Any topic under the computer science umbrella is welcome, published or not.
